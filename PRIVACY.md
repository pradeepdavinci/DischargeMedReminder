# Privacy

* The app does **not request the INTERNET permission** – it cannot send data anywhere.
* No accounts, analytics, advertising or Google Play services.
* Photos are taken with the phone's own camera app into the app's private cache and deleted
  automatically after a day. Text is read on the phone.
* Medicines, settings and the "taken" history are stored only in the app's private storage and
  are removed when the app is uninstalled.
* "Share schedule" sends text only to the app the user chooses.
