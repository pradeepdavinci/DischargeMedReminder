# Medical disclaimer

Discharge Med Reminder is a free, community-maintained reminder aid. It is **not a medical
device**, has not been clinically validated or approved by any regulator, and does not give
medical advice.

* Text recognition and sheet parsing can make mistakes. Every medicine must be checked against
  the printed discharge sheet before reminders are saved.
* If the app and the sheet, a doctor or a pharmacist disagree, follow the sheet / clinician.
* Phones can delay or block notifications (battery saving, Do Not Disturb, restarts, app
  updates). Do not rely on this app as the only safeguard for critical medicines.
* Use is entirely at your own risk; see the warranty disclaimer in LICENSE.

Hospitals or organisations that hand this app to patients are responsible for their own
clinical-safety, privacy and regulatory review.
